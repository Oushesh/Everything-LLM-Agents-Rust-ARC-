## LoopEng AI Coding Lab:
   You will build an analyst that looks for recurring problems in an agent's reviews and suggests changes to its instructions.

   Review its suggestion, decide whether to use it, and see how the results change

   The task agent and verifier are provided. You'll use the coding assistant to build the analyst.

   * Agent Loop: connect tool calls to the reports they produce
   * Verification Loop: examine feedback and the correctons it prompts.
   * Learning Loop: use saved reviews to support an instruction recommendation.

   * Event-driven loop: save your decision, trigger new reviews, and compare their quality.
